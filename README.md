TikSave is a high-speed, modern TikTok video, photo slide, and audio extractor application. Download TikTok videos without watermarks in pristine 8K 120FPS, HDR+ Ultra, 1080p Full HD, photo carousel slides, and extract studio-grade MP3 audio streams (Original Sound, High Sound Boost, VFX/Voice). Integrated with Gemini AI for instant viral captions, hooks, and hashtags ready to post on Facebook & TikTok.

### Features
- 🎵 **TikTok Audio Extractor**: Extract audio from any TikTok video with 3 sound profiles:
  - **Original Sound (320 kbps MP3)**
  - **High Sound Boost (+6dB Dynamic Volume)**
  - **VFX & Dialogue Voiceover Isolation**
- 📸 **TikTok Photo Slide Mode**: Download full carousel image slides in 8K/4K HDR+ resolution + soundtrack MP3.
- 💾 **Scoped Storage Compliant**: Zero EPERM errors, saves directly to Gallery (`Pictures/TikSave`), `Movies/TikSave`, and `Music/TikSave`.
- ✨ **AI Viral Creator Studio**: Instant viral hooks, ready-to-post Facebook descriptions, and high-reach hashtags (#fyp).
- 🚀 Ultra-fast dual-engine download (TikWM + Direct Stream fallback).
- 💧 Clean water-mark-free downloads in MP4 and MP3.
- 📺 In-app video & audio preview player.
- 📦 Single-file storage pipeline with MediaStore auto-sync.
- ⚡ 8K 120FPS & HDR+ support.
- 🔄 In-app auto-updater with background check against `version.json`.
